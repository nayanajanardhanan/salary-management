# PayScope — Salary Management System

A full-stack employee salary management application designed to help HR managers manage employee salary information and gain insights into organizational compensation.

## Overview

PayScope is a web-based salary management system that replaces spreadsheet-based salary management with a structured, maintainable, and user-friendly application.

The system enables HR managers to manage employee salary records, search and filter employee information, and analyze salary distribution across countries and departments.

## Problem Statement

Organizations managing salary information for thousands of employees across multiple countries often rely on spreadsheets, making salary management tedious and difficult to analyze.

PayScope aims to provide a centralized platform that simplifies employee salary management and helps HR managers understand how the organization compensates its employees.

## Planned Features

* Employee salary record management.
* Employee search and filtering.
* Pagination for employee records.
* Salary analytics and aggregation.
* Country-wise and department-wise insights.
* Seed script for 10,000 employees.
* Automated tests.
* Deployed web application.
